# Review: briefs of the invoices backend, the PostgreSQL invoice store and the test controls (2026-11-07)

- **Documents:** `docs/tasks/019`, `021`, `022`, `023`, `024` and the "Invoices and payments API" part of `docs/SPEC.md`. Task 022 was split in two (`022` request controls, `024` endpoints and reset) after the first pass.
- **Reviewer:** a second Claude process in the background, read-only, twice (a first pass over the briefs as written, a second pass over the rewritten ones). Every finding was checked against the real code before it was applied.
- **Result of the first pass:** 4 Critical and 13 Important, all confirmed. The Critical ones: the seed would have bumped the version of three seeded orders; the circular foreign key between orders and invoices made every reset fail; claiming the idempotency key before the locks deadlocked (the foreign key takes `FOR KEY SHARE`, so the invoice row is now locked `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` everywhere); the port could not store the response atomically with the payment.
- **Result of the second pass:** no Critical; 9 Important, all confirmed and fixed: `Invoice.payments` and `PaymentOutcome(response, replayed)`; the early fingerprint of a raw body can raise on `NaN`, non-JSON content and very deep bodies; no invoice wiring when a database is configured until task 021; `mark_paid` and `attach_invoice` as helpers on the open connection, never a second connection (self-deadlock); `ControlledClock` never reads the store when controls are disabled; one connection for the clock and the fault lookup; the gateway belongs to the store, not the service; the reset must not call stores that open their own transaction.
- **Also found while testing the supervisor's own lock timeout against a real PostgreSQL:** every login queues behind one advisory lock, so the default 5 second lock timeout turned a burst of 20 parallel logins into errors; that transaction now waits up to 30 seconds.
- **Not verified:** the lock behavior is from the documented conflict table and from reading, not from an experiment; the integration tests of tasks 021, 022 and 024 will be the experiment.
