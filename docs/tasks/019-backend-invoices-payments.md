## Requirements

1. **Models** (`models.py`): frozen dataclasses `Invoice` (`id`, `number`, `status: InvoiceStatus`, `order_id`, `order_number`, the customer snapshot `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `customer_tax_id`, `customer_address`, `lines: tuple[InvoiceLine, ...]` with the copied line fields, `subtotal`, `tax_rate`, `tax`, `total` as `Decimal`, `version`, `created_at`, `updated_at`), `InvoiceStatus` (`issued`, `paid`, `void`), `Payment` (`id`, `invoice_id`, `method`, `status`, `amount`, `reference`, `card_last4`, `reason`, `created_at`), and `StoredResponse` (`status_code: int`, `body: dict`) for idempotency. Never a card number or CVC.
2. **Ports** (`ports.py`), each method documented because a PostgreSQL adapter will be written from the text:
   - `InvoiceStore`: `list_invoices(*, query, status, order_id, page, page_size) -> tuple[list[Invoice], int]`; `get_invoice(id)`; `issue_invoice(order_id, expected_order_version, now) -> Invoice`; `register_payment(invoice_id, *, key, fingerprint, expected_version, attempt: PaymentAttempt, now) -> PaymentOutcome`; `void_for_order(order_id, now)`; `replace_all(invoices, payments)`. Exceptions: `InvoiceNotFound`, `OrderNotFound` (reuse the orders one), `VersionConflict`, and `ApiError`s for the business errors that the domain or the spec define (see point 4).
   - `issue_invoice` is atomic: it locks the order, then the customer (`FOR SHARE` in SQL), checks in the order of the spec (version, `INVOICE_ALREADY_EXISTS`, `ORDER_NOT_INVOICEABLE`, `CUSTOMER_TAX_ID_REQUIRED`), takes the next `FAC` number, copies the snapshots, stores the invoice and calls `attach_invoice` on the order.
   - `register_payment` is atomic and idempotent in one step: it locks the order first, then the invoice (SPEC section 5), and inside the lock (1) looks the key up (scoped to the invoice, remembered 24 hours): the same fingerprint replays the stored response and reports `replayed=True`, another fingerprint raises `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`; (2) otherwise checks the stale `version`, `INVOICE_NOT_PAYABLE` and `PAYMENT_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, in that order; (3) calls the gateway (a callable of the attempt, point 3) **while the locks are held**; (4) stores the payment row (`approved`, `declined` or `failed`); (5) when approved, sets the invoice to `paid` (`version` + 1) and calls `mark_paid` on the order; (6) stores the key with the response that the caller built, **only when a payment row was created**. A request rejected before step 4 (stale version, not payable, amount mismatch, a validation error) does not consume the key. In SQL the claim of the key is a unique `(invoice_id, key)` row inserted before the locks, so two concurrent requests with the same key give one payment and the second replays the first.
   - `void_for_order` voids the `issued` invoice of an order (`status` `void`, `version` + 1) and does nothing when there is none or when it is already final.
   - `PaymentGateway` Protocol (`gateway.py`): `charge(method: str, amount: Decimal, card_last4: str | None) -> GatewayDecision`, with a `FakeGateway` that wraps `gateway_decision`. `Mailer` Protocol (`mail.py`): `invoice_issued(to_email: str, number: str)`, with a `LoggingMailer` that writes one log line (the number only; never the address) and never raises.
3. **In-memory adapter** (`adapters/memory.py`), thread-safe through the shared lock (`main.py` creates one `RLock` and gives it to the order store and to the invoice store), holding the gateway it receives; the order store gets the invoice store as its `invoices` object. **Seed** (`seed.py`): `seed_invoices()` and `seed_payments()` with the exact data of `docs/SEED.md` (invoice `FAC-0001` issued for `PED-0003`, `FAC-0002` and `FAC-0003` paid, the four payments with their methods, statuses, reasons, references and `card_last4`, the fixed timestamps), then `attach_invoice` for the three orders; after seeding the next invoice is `FAC-0004` and the next payment id is 5.
4. **Service** (`service.py`): `InvoiceService(store, orders, clock, mailer, secret)` with `list_invoices`, `get`, `issue(order_id, expected_order_version)`, `pay(invoice_id, key, body, expected_version)`. `issue` calls the mailer **after** the store returns (a failure is logged and swallowed). `pay` validates, in the order of SPEC section 7: the invoice exists (404), the idempotency key (`validate_key`), the body (`method`, `amount`, `card` or `reference`, unknown fields, the card through `parse_card` with `today` from the application clock, the reference through `validate_transfer_reference`; field names `method`, `amount`, `card.number`, `card.expiry`, `card.cvc`, `reference`, `Idempotency-Key`), computes the fingerprint of the body with the card redacted, builds the response to store for each outcome (201 `PaymentResult`, 402 `PAYMENT_DECLINED` and 503 `PAYMENT_GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` with `payment_id` and `reason`) and returns either the created response or the replay. The amount compared with the total is quantized to 2 decimals. Version bounds, id bounds, `q` and paging follow the orders rules (copy them; do not edit the orders service).
5. **Schemas and routes** (`schemas.py`, `routes.py`), all **plain `def`**, under `/api` exactly as the "Invoices and payments API" table says; money fields are strings with 2 decimals; input models use `extra="forbid"` and strict integers; `POST /api/orders/{id}/invoice` is mounted by this module (the path parameter is `id`); permissions through `require_action(Action.CREATE_INVOICES)` and `REGISTER_PAYMENTS`; for routes with an id a dependency declared after the permission dependency loads the order or the invoice and raises the 404; `ETag` and `Location` as the spec says; the replay answers carry `Idempotency-Replayed: true`; the problem responses for the declined and failed payments carry the extra members `payment_id` and `reason` and the content type `application/problem+json`. `If-Match` is parsed as in the orders routes (copy the small function; more than 10 digits is rejected before converting).
6. **Order cancel** (`orders` module change b): cancelling a confirmed order voids its issued invoice in the same locked step; a test cancels an invoiced order and reads the invoice as `void`.
7. **Wiring** (`main.py`): one `RLock`, the order store (existing), the invoice store, the fake gateway, the logging mailer and the service, kept as `app.state.invoices`, `app.state.invoice_store`, `app.state.payment_gateway`; include the router.
8. **Tests** (`backend/tests/invoices/`), deterministic, module-scoped application and one login per role, a function-scoped autouse fixture that resets customers, orders and invoices to their seeds. Cover: contract-style classes for `InvoiceStore` taking a fixture that returns a fresh store (so the SQL adapter can reuse them): list filters and search, paging, issue (every error in order, the snapshot staying unchanged when the customer or the order's lines change later, the order's `invoice_id` and `version`), payment outcomes (approved: invoice and order `paid`; declined `4000 0000 0000 0002` and failed `4000 0000 0009 0003`: attempt stored, invoice and order unchanged; transfer and cash approved), the amount mismatch, not payable, stale version and `If-Match`, key replay (same body replays the stored response including a 402 and a 503, another body is `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`, a request rejected before the gateway does not consume the key, a declined attempt does), the 24-hour expiry of a key with the injected clock, void on cancel, `replace_all`, and threads: two concurrent payments with the same key and body give one payment and one replay; two concurrent payments with different keys on one invoice give exactly one approval. Routes for the four roles (finance and admin write; sales and read-only get 403 on writes; no cookie 401); validation of every field of the payment body at its boundaries (amount `"327.8"`, `"327.80"`, `"327.801"`, `"-1"`, `""`, a number; card number with spaces and hyphens, 15 and 17 digits, a failing Luhn; expiry `"13/30"`, `"1/30"`, the current month (valid through its last day) and last month; CVC `"12"` and `"1234"`; transfer reference of 3, 4, 40 and 41 characters; unknown fields; `card` with a transfer; an `Idempotency-Key` that is missing, empty, 65 characters and with a space); the order of checks (a wrong role with an unknown id gives 403; an unknown invoice gives 404 before any body error; a replay is decided before body validation; a stale version on a paid invoice gives 409 first); that no log record, response or stored value contains a card number or a CVC; the email notice is logged once per invoice and a failing mailer does not change the answer; the OpenAPI contains `Invoice`, `InvoiceSummary`, `Payment` and `PaymentResult` with every money field of type string; and the literal example bodies of the spec reproduced for one invoice.
9. **Docs.** An "Invoices and payments" section in `backend/README.md`: the layers, what a PostgreSQL adapter must guarantee (lock order, the unique key claim, one transaction for approve + invoice paid + order paid), the shared lock of the in-memory stores and example `curl` calls.
